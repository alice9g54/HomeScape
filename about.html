Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leaders

HomeScape was founded in 2011 with a simple vision: to make premium real estate accessible to Kenyan families and investors through transparency, integrity, and exceptional service.

What started as a small office in Westlands has grown into one of Kenya's most trusted real estate groups. We've helped over 500 families find their dream homes, guided countless investors toward profitable opportunities, and built lasting relationships based on trust.

Today, we continue to lead the market by embracing technology, investing in our team, and always putting our clients first.
